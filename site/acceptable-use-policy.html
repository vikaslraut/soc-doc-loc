Acceptable Use Policy

Effective: 21 November 2026Version: 1.0Provider: Riseaut Consulting

This policy applies to everyone who uses soc-doc-loc: society admins, owners, auditors, builders and their staff. It is part of the Terms of Service.

The principle

soc-doc-loc holds sensitive records of many families. Use it only for the lawful business of keeping and managing a society's or project's records, and in a way that is safe for others. The Customer is responsible for the acts of everyone it gives access to.

Use it for

  • Maintaining statutory registers, member and flat records, meeting, election and nomination records, notices, investments and the society's documents.
  • Managing a society's public website and sending reminders and notices to its own members.
  • Capturing and handing over a builder's project documents to a society.

You must not

Unlawful or harmful content

  • Upload or publish anything unlawful, defamatory, obscene, threatening, or that infringes someone's intellectual property or privacy.
  • Enter personal data you have no right or lawful basis to hold, or enter someone's identity numbers for a purpose unrelated to the society's records.
  • Upload malware or anything designed to damage, disable or overload the Service or others' systems.

Misuse of access

  • Share sign-in emails or codes, sign in as someone else, or allow a person to keep admin access after they have left office.
  • Access or try to access another society's data, or data your role does not allow.
  • Bypass, disable or test the limits of security controls, role permissions, identity masking, the audit log or the ban on bulk export of member data.
  • Scrape, copy at scale, or extract member data by automated means, or take screenshots or copies of other people's masked or identity data to share it.

Misuse of the Service

  • Resell, sub-license, white-label or offer the Service to others as your own, or use it to build a competing product.
  • Reverse-engineer, decompile or copy the software, except where law allows it despite this clause.
  • Send spam, unsolicited commercial messages or messages unrelated to the society's affairs through the Service's email or reminders.
  • Alter, backdate or falsify records, including the audit log, to mislead members, auditors or authorities.
  • Use the Service for anything that is illegal in India.

Security testing

Do not run penetration, load or vulnerability tests against the Service without our written permission. If you find a vulnerability, report it to socdocloc@gmail.com and give us reasonable time to fix it before telling others; do not access, change or keep data that is not yours.

Your content

You are responsible for what you upload and publish, including on your society's public website. Do not put private identity data or confidential documents on public pages. We do not monitor content in advance and are not responsible for it, but we may review it when we receive a complaint or have reason to think this policy has been broken, with as little access to content as necessary.

What we may do

If we think this policy is broken, we may, as we judge appropriate and proportionate: ask you to fix it; remove or disable the content; limit or suspend a user, an account or the whole Service; end the agreement under the Terms of Service; and report it to the authorities or help them where the law requires or permits. For serious or urgent risks we may act before telling you. Fees already paid are not refunded for use that breaks this policy.

Reporting a problem

Tell us at socdocloc@gmail.com if you see misuse, think your account has been compromised or want content removed. Give the society's address, what you saw and any links. We will review and respond.

Changes

We may update this policy to keep up with new risks or law. The date above shows the latest version, and continued use means you accept it.
