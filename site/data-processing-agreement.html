Scope and roles

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service between Riseaut Consulting, a Udyam-registered micro enterprise (Udyam Registration No. UDYAM-MH-26-1152368), of D304, Park59, Wakad Block D, Kaspate Chowk, Pimpri Chinchwad, Pune 411057, Maharashtra, India ("Processor", "we") and the Customer ("Fiduciary"). It governs personal data in Customer Data that we process for the Customer.

The Customer is the Data Fiduciary and we are its Data Processor under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules ("Data Protection Law"). Where another law applies to a person (for example the GDPR for a person in the EU), the parties will treat the Customer as controller and us as processor on the same terms to the extent required. This DPA does not apply to data we hold for our own purposes; that is covered by the Privacy Policy.

Our obligations

We will:

  1. process personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions, which are these Terms, this DPA and the Customer's use and configuration of the Service, unless the law requires otherwise, in which case we will tell the Customer first if the law permits;
  2. not sell the data, use it for our own marketing or profiling, combine it with other data, or use it to train AI models;
  3. make sure our personnel who handle it are bound by confidentiality and have access only as needed;
  4. apply the security measures in Annexure 1;
  5. tell the Customer if we believe an instruction breaches Data Protection Law, and we may pause carrying it out until it is clarified.

The Customer's obligations

The Customer confirms that it has a lawful basis, and the notices and consents Data Protection Law requires, to put personal data into the Service, and that its instructions comply with law. The Customer decides what to record, who has admin, owner and auditor access, and how long to keep data. It is responsible for responding to requests and complaints from its members and owners, with our help under clause 7. It will not ask us to do anything that the Acceptable Use Policy forbids.

Sub-processors

The Customer gives general authorisation for the sub-processors in Annexure 2. We will bind each to data protection terms no less protective than this DPA and remain responsible for their performance of those obligations.

We will give the Customer at least 15 days' notice (by email or a notice on this page) before adding or replacing a sub-processor. The Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within that period. If we cannot resolve the objection, the Customer may end the affected subscription and we will refund the unused part of the prepaid fee for the remaining term.

Security and breach

We keep the measures in Annexure 1 or measures that are no less protective. If we become aware of a breach of security that leads to accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, disclosure of or access to Customer personal data, we will notify the Customer's admin email without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours of confirming the breach. The notice will say what we know about what happened, the data and people affected, steps taken and a contact for follow-up. We will take reasonable steps to contain and remedy it and help the Customer meet its notification duties to the Data Protection Board and affected people. The Customer decides whether and how to notify them, unless the law makes us do it. Our notice is not an admission of fault.

Assistance

Taking into account the nature of the Service, we will provide reasonable help, through the Service's own features or on written request, to let the Customer respond to requests to access, correct or erase personal data, and with its data-protection impact assessments and consultations with authorities. Reasonable help that is more than a minor effort may be charged at our then-current rates, which we will quote in advance. Requests that reach us directly from a data subject will be passed to the Customer, and we will not respond except to say we have done so.

Audit

On request, not more than once a year, we will give the Customer information reasonably needed to show we comply with this DPA, such as a written summary of our security measures. If that is not enough, the Customer may audit us, at its own cost, with at least 30 days' notice, during business hours, under confidentiality, by an independent auditor who is not our competitor, at a time and in a manner that does not harm our other Customers or the security of the Service. The audit will not include other Customers' data or our source code. It may be repeated only after a confirmed breach.

Location of data

Customer data is hosted in India (AWS Asia Pacific, Mumbai). We will not transfer it outside India, except where a sub-processor needs limited access for support and security purposes, or where the Customer asks us to, and always in accordance with Data Protection Law. Any restriction notified by the Central Government under the DPDP Act will be followed.

Return and deletion

When the subscription ends, the Customer may request a full export of Customer Data on the terms in the Terms of Service, including the charge of ₹2 per MB. We keep Customer Data for 90 days after the end, then delete it from live systems and let it age out of backups within a further 90 days, unless the law requires us to keep it, in which case we will protect it and use it only for that purpose. On request, we will confirm deletion in writing.

Liability and order of precedence

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limits in the Terms of Service, and amounts paid under this DPA count toward the same cap. If this DPA conflicts with the Terms of Service about personal data, this DPA prevails. It continues for as long as we hold Customer personal data. Disputes are resolved as set out in the Terms of Service (arbitration seated in Pune; courts at Pune).

Annexure 1: Security measures

  • Tenant isolation. Each society has its own database; access is resolved per society at the connection level.
  • Access control. Email one-time-code sign-in; role-based permissions (admin, owner, auditor); identity fields (PAN, phone, email, Aadhaar) masked on the server for roles that do not need them.
  • Encryption. Data is encrypted in transit; storage at the cloud provider uses encryption at rest.
  • Audit trail. Data changes are recorded in an append-only audit log protected at database level and chained to detect tampering.
  • Data minimisation. Bulk export of member data is disabled in the application; secrets and identity numbers are kept out of logs.
  • Infrastructure. Hosted on AWS in India; infrastructure managed as code; production changes are controlled and reviewed.
  • Backups and recovery. Regular database backups with recovery procedures; objectives are in the Service Level Agreement.
  • Personnel. Access limited to those who need it, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Testing. Automated tests and security review of code changes before release.
