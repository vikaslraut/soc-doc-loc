From paper to permanent

A digital replica of every register, ledger and member record.

Most co-operative housing societies still keep their registers by hand. Ink fades, pages tear, cupboards get cleared out and secretaries move on. A society cannot keep its records searchable and intact for long on paper.

soc-doc-loc digitises all of it — a faithful, structured copy of every official document your society is required to maintain, kept safe for every committee that comes after yours.

Class & audit

Wear the medal of pride. Earn your Class A audit certificate.

Under the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act and the model bye-laws, every society must maintain a prescribed set of accounts, registers and records. Statutory auditors grade societies A, B, C or D based on how complete the records are, how promptly accounts are kept, internal controls, member compliance and adherence to the bye-laws.

A Class A grade signals a well-run society, one whose books, minutes and member records stand up to scrutiny. soc-doc-loc gives office bearers one organised place for every required record, and gives auditors a clean, read-only trail to review.
