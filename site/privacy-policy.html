Who we are and our two roles

soc-doc-loc (the "Service") is operated by Riseaut Consulting, a Udyam-registered micro enterprise (Udyam Registration No. UDYAM-MH-26-1152368), of D304, Park59, Wakad Block D, Kaspate Chowk, Pimpri Chinchwad, Pune 411057, Maharashtra, India ("we", "us"). It is a records and document repository for co-operative housing societies and resident welfare associations in Maharashtra, and a handover tool for builders and developers.

We act in two different roles, and this matters for who is responsible for what:

  • As a processor, for society and builder data. A housing society or builder that subscribes (the "Customer") decides what member, owner, nominee and document data goes into its own space and why. The Customer is the "Data Fiduciary" under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"); we process that data only on the Customer's behalf under our Data Processing Agreement.
  • As a fiduciary, for our own relationship data. For website visitors, people who contact us, and the account details we hold to run billing and support, we decide the purpose ourselves and this policy applies directly.

If you are a flat owner or member and want to see, correct or erase your data held in a society's space, please ask your society's office-bearers first; they decide how that data is used. We will help them respond.

What we collect

Data entered into a Customer's space

  • Member and owner details: name, flat and wing, contact numbers, email, address, PAN and Aadhaar (where the Customer records them), share-certificate and membership details, nominations and nominee details.
  • Society records: registers, meeting and election records, agreements, investments, notices, documents and files uploaded by the Customer.
  • Builder handover records: checklist items, possession and parking documents, and notes.
  • An audit log of who changed what and when.

Data about people who sign in

  • Email address and role (admin, owner or auditor), one-time sign-in codes (which expire and are not stored in readable form), and session information.

Data about visitors and enquiries

  • Name, email, phone and message when you email, call or WhatsApp us or ask for a demo.
  • Basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages used, to keep the Service secure and to understand which features are used. We use this in aggregate and do not build advertising profiles.

We do not knowingly collect data of children for our own purposes. A Customer may record details of a minor nominee or a member's dependants where a statutory register requires it; that data stays under the Customer's control.

Who we share it with

We do not share a Customer's data with third parties for their own purposes. We use a small number of infrastructure providers who process data only to run the Service for us ("sub-processors"). They are listed in Annexure 2 of the Data Processing Agreement and currently include Amazon Web Services (hosting, storage and email delivery).

We may disclose data where required by law, court order or a lawful request from a competent authority, and to protect our rights or the security of the Service. Where permitted, we will tell the affected Customer first. If our business is sold or merged, data may transfer to the successor, which must honour this policy.

Inside a society's space, who sees what is decided by roles. Admins and auditors can see full identity details; other members see co-owners' email, phone and PAN masked, and Aadhaar is shown only by its last four digits. This masking is applied on our servers, not just on screen.

Where data is kept and how it is protected

  • Location. Production data is hosted with Amazon Web Services in the Asia Pacific (Mumbai) region, India. Each society's records are held in a separate database.
  • Protection. Encrypted connections; email one-time-code sign-in; role-based access enforced on the server; masking of identity fields; a tamper-evident, append-only audit log; and bulk export of member data is disabled in the application.
  • Logging. We do not write PAN, Aadhaar, sign-in codes or connection secrets to our logs.
  • Limits. No system is perfectly secure. We use measures that are reasonable for the nature of the data, but we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your sign-in email account secure and tell us at once if you suspect misuse.

If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting you or a Customer, we will notify the Customer, and the Data Protection Board of India and affected individuals where the DPDP Act requires it, in the manner and time it prescribes.

How long we keep it

  • Customer data is kept for as long as the Customer's subscription is active. After the subscription ends we keep it for 90 days so the Customer can request an export (see the Terms of Service), then delete it from live systems. Encrypted backups age out within a further 90 days.
  • Audit-log and billing records may be kept longer where law requires, for example tax records for the period the law specifies.
  • Enquiries that do not become a subscription are deleted within 24 months of the last contact.

Societies have their own statutory duties to preserve certain registers. Deleting a record in the Service does not discharge those duties; keeping a lawful copy is the Customer's responsibility.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you may ask to access a summary of your personal data and the identities of those it is shared with, ask for correction or erasure, withdraw consent where processing rests on consent, nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated, and have your grievance heard.

Write to socdocloc@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request". We may need to verify who you are. We aim to respond within 30 days. For data held in a society's space, we will pass your request to the society and help it respond.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is constituted and the complaint process is notified.
