Availability target

We aim to make the production Service available 99.0% of the time in each calendar month ("Uptime Target"). This is a best-effort target for a service run by a small team; it is not a guarantee of uninterrupted use.

Monthly Uptime % = (total minutes in the month − minutes of Downtime) ÷ total minutes in the month × 100.

"Downtime" means minutes in which the Customer's users cannot sign in to the Service or open its records, as measured by our own monitoring, excluding the items in the next clause.

What does not count as downtime

  • Planned maintenance of up to 8 hours in a month. We try to give at least 24 hours' notice by email or on the Service and to schedule it at night (IST) or on Sundays. Emergency security maintenance may be done with shorter notice.
  • Problems caused by the Customer or its users: wrong data, misuse, breach of the Acceptable Use Policy, or their own devices, networks, browsers or email accounts, including delay in delivery of sign-in emails by the user's email provider.
  • Failures of the public internet, telecom carriers, or third-party services and infrastructure that are outside our reasonable control, and events beyond our reasonable control such as natural events, power failure, war, government action, labour disputes or cyber attacks despite reasonable precautions.
  • Suspension or limits applied under the Terms of Service or the Acceptable Use Policy.
  • Beta, trial or sandbox spaces, and features marked as such.
  • Slowness that does not stop use; individual features (for example PDF generation or email reminders) being delayed while the rest of the Service works.

Service credits

If the Monthly Uptime % falls below the Uptime Target, the Customer may claim a credit:

Monthly Uptime %Credit
Below 99.0% but at least 97.0%5% of one-twelfth of the annual fee
Below 97.0%10% of one-twelfth of the annual fee
  • To claim, email socdocloc@gmail.com within 30 days after the end of the month, naming the dates and times and how the problem affected you. We will check against our records and reply within 15 business days.
  • Credits are applied to the next invoice. They have no cash value and are not paid out, except for a final month when the agreement ends, where we will refund the credit if you have no later invoice.
  • Credits are capped at 10% of one-twelfth of the annual fee for any one month.
  • Credits are the sole and exclusive remedy for any failure to meet the Uptime Target or other service level in this agreement. Credits are available only if the Customer's fees are paid up.

Backups and recovery

We take regular backups of Customer databases and documents and test our ability to restore them from time to time. Our objective is to restore service and data after a failure as quickly as reasonably possible. These are objectives, not guarantees, and a restore may lose changes made since the last backup. The Customer should keep its own copies of any records it must preserve by law and may request a paid export at any time under the Terms of Service.

Notices and status

We will tell the Customer's admin by email about planned maintenance and about significant incidents that affect it, and will give a short explanation once they are resolved. We do not currently run a public status page; if we start one, we will link it from this site.

Scope and changes

This agreement covers the production Service for paying Customers. It does not cover onboarding, data migration or training, which are done on a best-effort basis. We may improve or update these service levels, but will not make them materially worse for a Customer during its paid term without that Customer's consent.
